September 2026 monthly performance update | Prepared October 1

September establishes the monthly baseline.

This is the first Avid Reader update built around a clean monthly reporting window. September combined consistent short-form discovery with stronger long-form depth, while the production system expanded across Antonio Damasio, Sam Roberts, Lisa Crites, archive repurposing, brand infrastructure, and the next Sam Kean campaign.

Executive summary

YouTube generated 17,079 views and 312 hours 27 minutes of watch time during September. Shorts supplied 69.6% of views, while long-form supplied 89.1% of watch time.

Instagram generated 12,285 views, with 89.9% coming from non-followers. The strategic opportunity is increasingly clear: discovery is working. The next job is converting that attention into full interviews, followers, and book sales.

Reporting note: YouTube figures cover September 1–30, 2026. Instagram uses the supplied current 30-day Insights view. Previous reports used broader or mixed platform windows, so September is treated as the clean monthly baseline going forward rather than forcing a misleading month-over-month comparison.

A dedicated conversion framework was created around clip selection, winning the first 1–2 seconds, curiosity gaps, burned-in captions, b-roll, and explicit routing into the full interview. This strategy is now supported by the September format-crossover data.

The channel also published the 3DPrintopia feature and experimental AI content featuring Sam. The AI Short became the month's most-viewed YouTube Short, providing a useful signal that selective creative experimentation can broaden discovery without replacing the core author-interview format.

October priorities

Double down on conversion, not just volume.

Run the Lisa Crites campaign as the October priority.

Push more Short viewers into the full interview.
Use related-video links, pinned comments, descriptions, on-screen end cards, and stronger verbal bridges. The 26% crossover rate gives us a clear monthly benchmark to improve.

Use older high-value interviews between new campaigns so publishing stays consistent without forcing low-quality new production.

Judge clips not only by views but by stayed-to-watch rate, subscribers, profile activity, interview traffic, and book-link actions.

Advance Sam Kean and prepare upcoming guests earlier.

Track book intent more directly.
Where possible, use campaign-specific links or landing pages so social traffic can be tied to Wellington Square Bookshop actions rather than stopping at platform engagement.

Methodology notes

September is treated as the first clean monthly baseline. Earlier client reports used different or broader reporting windows, so this report does not manufacture month-over-month percentage changes from mismatched periods.

YouTube changed Shorts view counting on August 27, 2026. Shorts views are now counted when a Short starts or replays, which may affect comparisons with older reports.

Instagram's supplied follower-notification feed is not assumed to be exhaustive. Visible September follows are treated as qualitative support only, while 976 is reported as the current follower count at capture.
