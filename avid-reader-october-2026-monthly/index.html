01   /   YouTube

Where the growth came from

Views rose 160.6% from September and watch time rose 45.0%. I came across that 52-second Tesla Roadster clip one evening. I thought you’d get a kick out of it, and I had a feeling people would share it. I posted it on October 15 as a one-off. It earned 280 shares.

The remaining uploads drew 21,127 views and 366.2 watch hours. The entire channel recorded 17,079 views and 312.5 hours in September. October finished with 57 subscribers gained and a net change of zero.

Discovery

Barrat led the Shorts

“Is AI Doom a Marketing Strategy?” drew 1,169 views. Across our Shorts, 50% stayed to watch, up from 45.91% in September. The Shorts feed supplied 84.5% of Shorts views.

Interview viewing

Damasio held attention

People spent 42.3 hours watching your full conversation with Antonio Damasio across 487 views. The episode averaged 7:08 per view, compared with 1:55 for the channel’s Videos category.

Format mix

51 Shorts and 5 Videos

Shorts supplied 36.2% of views and Videos supplied 63.8%. YouTube classified the Roadster upload as a Video. Browse features supplied 36.0% of channel views, ahead of the Shorts feed at 30.5% and search at 23.7%.

More YouTube figures and date windows

Studio showed 28.3K Videos views and 16.1K Shorts views, including 7.6K engaged Shorts views. Videos had a 5.7% thumbnail click-through rate. Across the channel, 162.6K thumbnail impressions yielded a 5.1% click-through rate.

In Studio’s separate rolling 28-day returning-viewer panel, 42% watched Videos only, 39% watched Shorts only and 19% watched both. September’s report recorded 26% watching both, but its audience and dates could not be matched for a direct comparison.

02   /   Instagram

The Vagus Nerve led the Reels

Damasio’s Reel led the supplied rankings with about 2,900 views, 155 interactions and 20 shares. Across the account, 95% of views came from non-followers. Instagram recorded 14,274 viewers and 441 accounts engaged.

The profile recorded 168 visits and two external link taps. In November, I want to see how many of those visitors make it to the book page at Wellington.
