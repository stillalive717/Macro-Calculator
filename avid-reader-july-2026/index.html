Performance

Three platforms, three different jobs

Instagram

340,256 views

261,703 accounts reached, 37,781 interactions, 23,146 accounts engaged, and 661 profile visits. Approximately 99.5% of views came from non-followers.

Breakout Reel: “How to Write Any Ad” generated 349,670 views, 15,155 saves, 6,069 shares, and 309 follows.

YouTube

29,842 views

298.2 watch hours during July 3–30. Shorts generated 27,543 views and 98.4 watch hours. Long-form generated 2,321 views and 199.8 watch hours.

Strategic signal: Shorts drove discovery, while long-form delivered depth and most of the watch time.

TikTok

9,400 views

40 profile views, 356 likes, 18 comments, and 8 shares during the available July reporting window.

Strategic signal: activity was measurable, but return on production effort remained weakest here.

August priorities

Finish, pause, test, and measure

1. Finish the queue

Publish the completed Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube content through August 15 so finished work is not wasted.

2. Pause new TikTok production

Keep the account live, but stop creating additional TikTok-specific content after the prepared queue ends.

3. Test Facebook Reels

Run a controlled 30-day Facebook test using the existing short-form asset library, then compare reach, engagement, and meaningful actions.

Primary channels: Maintain Instagram for discovery and YouTube for both discovery and long-form depth.
